The Idea

My suburb has a stray cat problem. Every mating season, the noise is unbearable. This is my formal (but fun) take on it.

About This Project

Built while following a course on Frontend Masters, I took the brief and made it my own by crafting a fake political conference website for the cats of Guildford, complete with distinguished speakers, official-sounding committees, and one very opinionated Chief Treat Auditor (my own cat, Tippy Morris Jr.).

The landing page features a kitten video and a "Buy Ticket" button that plays a meow sound on hover.

The Speakers page lists each delegate with their credentials, area of expertise, and a portrait taken with full diplomatic seriousness (ish).

It's funny. It's functional. And it taught me firsthand how to build and deploy a multi-page HTML/CSS site from scratch, host it on Netlify, and write copy that makes people smile.

What I Practised

Multi-page site structure and navigation, responsive layout, hover interactions with JavaScript, and end-to-end deployment via GitHub and Netlify.

I believe the real skill here was taking a course exercise and transforming it into something with a distinct voice and concept.

The next biggest learning for me during this project was responsive design. I had to make sure the site looked good and functioned well on different screen sizes, which involved a lot of testing and tweaking of the CSS. It was a great exercise in thinking about how users interact with a site on various devices and ensuring a seamless experience across the board.
