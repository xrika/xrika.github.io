Internal Service Design

A self-initiated service design project built from the inside. Designed with zero budget and zero software-install, this design is still running untouched across three departments over a year later. Working across E-commerce Operations and Customer Experience for over three years, I lived inside the broken system long enough to know exactly where it was failing.
  • Role: Systems Designer & Operations Liaison
  • Problem: Fragmented communication between remote CX and on-site fulfillment led to "ghost" reservations, lost inventory, and customer churn.

  • Workflow Improvements
  • Interdepartmental Communications
